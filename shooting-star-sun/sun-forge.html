The shader (GLSL) + how to port it to Minecraft 

    

      
This is the whole sun. Paste this fragment shader into your mod. Three things change when it runs in Minecraft:

      
1. Replace the fixed camera (ro, rd) with the engine's real one, built from CamFwd / CamUp / CamRight — the same uniforms the demo mod uses.

      
2. Set sunC from the sun's world position relative to the camera, and drive its y from your cutscene clock t so it rises out of the hole.

      
3. Render to an RGBA16F target and add a bloom pass — that turns the >1.0 HDR values into the searing glow. For the photo modes, bind your 4K NASA JPEG as uTex exactly like the mod binds its planet maps.
